## 

shoving code out the door was never the problem

professionalism

understanding the difference in understanding between people

condensing what is important to know vs. interesting to know

communicating and breaking down abstract concepts

incentivising people into good behavior (star trek principle - environment where resistance is taxing and it's just easier to behave)

we always had an industry that struggled with people. We focus on "the death of the junior" but seniors are dying too

Conculsion:
personal annecdote about being at Quotech, couple of years of "pure IC" - now trying to shift gears into people

it used to be okay to measure seniority by the ability to produce working software/work independently, but we need to realise that doesn't automatically bring the soft skills anymore
